<h4><strong>Lantern Displays</strong></h4>
<p>📅 19 September – 4 October 2026</p>
<p>📍Lakeside Field, Lakeside Garden | Cloud Pagoda &amp; Cleansing Biotope
Ponds, Chinese Garden | Floral Garden &amp; Resthouse Pond, Japanese Garden</p>
<p>⏰6:30pm – 10:30pm daily</p>
<h4><strong>📍Lakeside Field, Lakeside Garden</strong></h4>
<div data-type="detailGroup" class="isomer-accordion isomer-accordion-white">
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Scene 1 - The Spirit Awakens (千年化形)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>After a thousand years of cultivation, the white snake spirit Bai Suzhen
finally gains a human form. Together with her companion Xiao Qing, she
leaves the mountains to explore the human world. A powerful spirit Bai
Suzhen may be, but she is soft-spoken and good-natured. She is intrigued
by human matters, such as emotions, day-to-day living, and the unpredictability
of life. Little does she know, venturing into the human world will change
her destiny forever.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Scene 2 - Fateful Encounter on the Bridge (断桥情缘)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>On a rainy day, Bai Suzhen meets Xu Xian, a humble scholar, at Broken
Bridge in Hangzhou, and he offers to share his umbrella with her. This
small act of kindness sparks off a bond between the two – Xu Xian is drawn
to her elegance and warmth, while Bai Suzhen is touched by his honesty
and sincerity. In that quiet moment, the threads of fate intertwine, weaving
their lives together.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Scene 3 - A Mortal Life Together (人间相守)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>Bai Suzhen and Xu Xian marry and settle into a peaceful life in Hangzhou.
They start a small medicine shop and help the sick, enjoying their days
together. This period marks the peak of their happiness, underlined by
mutual understanding, trust, and loving companionship. Bai Suzhen learns
more about human emotions, while Xu Xian experiences love that seems both
ordinary and extraordinary at the same time.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Scene 4 - The Realgar Wine Revelation (雄黄现形)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>During the Dragon Boat Festival, the head abbot of Jinshan Temple, Fa
Hai, warns Xu Xian that Bai Suzhen is not human. The confused and anxious
Xu Xian gives her realgar wine, which is believed to make a spirit revert
to its true form. Unable to resist its effects, Bai Suzhen reveals her
original snake form. Shocked and terrified, Xu Xian is unable to accept
that the woman he loves is actually a snake. In an instant, his love turns
into fear and dismay.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Scene 5 - The Battle at Jinshan Temple (金山寺之战)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>Desperate to reunite with Xu Xian, Bai Suzhen confronts Fa Hai at Jinshan
Temple. In this fierce battle, she unleashes her great powers, summoning
waters to engulf the temple. However, Fa Hai manages to overcome her using
his superior powers and divine blessings. Though driven by her deep love
for Xu Xian, Bai Suzhen is ultimately defeated, and the couple remains
separated.</p>
</div>
</details>
</div>
<h4><strong>📍Cloud Pagoda &amp; Cleansing Biotope Ponds, Chinese Garden</strong></h4>
<div data-type="detailGroup" class="isomer-accordion isomer-accordion-white">
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Cleansing Biotope Ponds - Moonlit Reunion (水上嫦娥)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>After ascending to the Moon, Chang’e gazes upon the mortal world from
her celestial home. On this tranquil night, she stands quietly, surrounded
by blooming lotus flowers that symbolise purity and hope. Her gentle presence
serves as a reminder that while our loved ones may be far away from us,
we remain connected under the same moon – may the moonlight bear witness
for an earlier reunion with those we miss dearly.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Cleansing Biotope Ponds - Whispers of the Jade Rabbit (玉兔送福)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>Legend tells of the Jade Rabbit that faithfully accompanies Chang’e in
the palace on the Moon, where she spends eternity preparing celestial elixirs.
Over here, the Jade Rabbit and its friends have come together to spread
cheer, hoping to bring joy, luck, abundance, and peace to everyone.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Cloud Pagoda - The Legend of the White Snake (白蛇传)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>The enduring tale of Bai Suzhen, Xu Xian, Xiao Qing, and Fa Hai has touched
countless hearts with its themes of love, sacrifice, and redemption. In
this reimagined story ending, the four characters are reunited before the
pagoda, setting aside past grievances to embrace forgiveness and harmony.
Their reunion symbolises that openness and love can surpass any challenge,
allowing all to enjoy hope and peace.</p>
</div>
</details>
</div>
<h4><strong>📍Floral Garden &amp; Resthouse Pond, Japanese Garden</strong></h4>
<div data-type="detailGroup" class="isomer-accordion isomer-accordion-white">
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Floral Garden - Kaguya's Moon Garden</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>Bathed in the glow of the full moon, Princess Kaguya radiates elegance
and tranquility. Inspired by Japanese folktale, she is surrounded by blooming
flowers, playful rabbits, and drifting clouds, creating a dreamlike scene
that celebrates beauty, peace, and the magic of moonlit nights.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>Resthouse Pond - Waters of Abundance (锦鲤送福)</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>Beneath the glow of floating lanterns, colourful koi glide through shimmering
waters in a graceful dance of light and movement. Admired for their beauty,
resilience, and calm strength, the koi symbolise prosperity, harmony, and
the continuous flow of good fortune. This tranquil scene invites you to
pause, reflect, and embrace the promise of brighter days ahead.</p>
</div>
</details>
</div>
