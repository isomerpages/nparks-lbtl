<p>Steeped in history and beauty, the Chinese Garden comes alive this Mid-Autumn
with a rich array of programmes for the whole family. Follow the Legend
of the White Snake through stunning lantern displays and a light show at
the Cloud Pagoda, catch live performances at the Ficus Lawn, and explore
the Bonsai Garden with hands-on activities, storytelling sessions, and
Hanfu dress-up. Keep an eye out for the dazzling LED lion dances, and don't
miss Xiao Qing's stamp rally as she winds her way through the gardens on
a very special delivery mission.</p>
