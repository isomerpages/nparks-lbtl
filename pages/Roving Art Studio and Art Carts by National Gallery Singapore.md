<p><strong>Art Studio: Create an Artwork using Mooncake Stamps!</strong>
</p>
<p>Inspired by Georgette Chen’s <em>Still Life (Moon Festival Table), </em>visitors
will use actual mooncake stamps, similar to the one in the artwork, to
stamp on the activity sheets.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Art Cart: Kampong Life with Liu Kang</strong>
</p>
<p>Inspired by Life by the River by Liu Kang, this art cart brings to life
the vibrant kampong scenes — where riverside villages were alive with community
spirit.</p>
<p>Explore the artwork up close to discover the simple joys of kampong life
and reflect on Singapore’s rich cultural tapestry during this season of
reunion and gratitude.</p>
<p>Visitors can also view a miniature kampong model and imagine families
gathering and celebrating harmony with loved ones and nature.</p>
