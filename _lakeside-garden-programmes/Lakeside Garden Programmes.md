<p>Get ready for an evening of festive fun, creativity and discovery at Lakeside
Garden! Gather the family to solve lantern riddles, unleash your inner
artist at the Roving Art Studio, and groove to vibrant live performances
from local artistes. Light up the night on the Mediacorp Lantern Trail,
let the little ones bounce and play to their hearts’ content, or feast
on irresistible local and international favourites at the Food Market.
With glowing lanterns, lively music, hands-on experiences and plenty of
family-friendly thrills, Lakeside Garden is the place to be for an unforgettable
Mid-Autumn celebration.</p>
