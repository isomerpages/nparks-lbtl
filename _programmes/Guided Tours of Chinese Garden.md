<p>Immerse yourself in the rich history and serene beauty of the Chinese
Garden. In this guided tour, explore the intricately designed landscapes,
vibrant flora, and traditional architectural marvels.</p>
<p>We are committed to creating an inclusive and accessible environment for
everyone. A certified sign language interpreter will be present for selected
sessions on 28 Sep, 5 and 12 Oct. We warmly welcome and encourage participation
from our Deaf and hard of hearing community members.</p>
<p>Register <a href="https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/events" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a> by
searching ‘LBTL 2025’ and selecting your desired session!</p>
<p><strong>Date</strong>: 27, 28 Sep, and 3, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12 Oct 2025
<br><strong>Time: </strong>5pm – 6:30pm
<br><strong>Location: </strong>Chinese Garden (meeting point: Entrance Pavilion,
Lakeside Garden)</p>
