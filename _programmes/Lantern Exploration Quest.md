<h4><strong>Lantern Exploration Quest 🔍</strong></h4>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 80%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Activities/NEAR_app___JLG_pins.png">
</div>
<p>Find scenes from "Sun Wukong's Journey to the West" lantern displays throughout
Jurong Lake Gardens during the Lights by the Lake 2025 event, capture memorable
moments with the NParks Explore A Route (NEAR) mobile app and take home
exclusive Jurong Lake Gardens magnetic pins!</p>
<p><strong><u>How to Participate</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Start your journey by downloading the NEAR app and creating an account.</p>
<p>Navigate to the Events bubble or Events screen within the NEAR app and
select "Lights by the Lake 2025 – Lantern Exploration Quest" to begin your
adventure.</p>
<p>Discover and photograph all five scenes from "Sun Wukong's Journey to
the West" lantern displays throughout Jurong Lake Gardens during the Lights
by the Lake 2025 event. Three scenes await you at Lakeside Field, whilst
the remaining two can be found at Butterfly Field.</p>
<p>For each scene you discover, take a photograph and post it to the NEAR
app's Community Wall. Complete all five photo quests to be eligible for
the redemption!</p>
<p><strong><u>Redemption</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Upon completing all five photo quests, participants can redeem a limited
edition Jurong Lake Gardens magnetic pin.</p>
<p>Visit any of the three designated Jurong Lake Gardens Visitor Services
Counters to collect your prize. Redemptions are available daily between
6:30 PM and 10:00 PM only.</p>
<p><strong>Visitor Services Counter Locations:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Entrance Pavilion (North Carpark)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Chinese Garden (Pagoda Plaza)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Japanese Garden (Water Lily Pavilion)</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Important Notes</strong> 
</p>
<p>Each NEAR app account is entitled to one limited edition magnetic pin
only, available whilst stocks last.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Make the most of your Lights by the Lake experience <strong><a href="https://near.nparks.gov.sg/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">download the NEAR app</a></strong> now
and embark on your adventure! Access essential event information including
points of interest, food vendors, key details, and helpful wayfinding to
car parks and MRT stations.</p>
