<p>📅 19 September – 27 September 2026</p>
<p>📍 Sunken Garden, Japanese Garden | Cloud Pagoda, Chinese Garden</p>
<h4><strong>Sunken Garden</strong></h4>
<p>⏰7:30pm – 10:30pm</p>
<p>A vibrant wash of multicoloured lights transforms the Sunken Garden into
a magical after-dark spectacle, dotted with delicate lotus lanterns that
add a touch of festive warmth.</p>
<h4><strong>Cloud Pagoda</strong></h4>
<p>⏰ 7:30pm – 10pm, every 30 minutes (last show at 10pm)</p>
<p>Watch the Legend of the White Snake come to life in a stunning light show
at the Cloud Pagoda. In this reimagined ending, Bai Suzhen, Xu Xian, Xiao
Qing, and Fa Hai are finally reunited — a beautiful reminder that love
and forgiveness can overcome all. On 21 September, the Cloud Pagoda will
also glow in yellow and purple in honour of World Alzheimer's Day.</p>
