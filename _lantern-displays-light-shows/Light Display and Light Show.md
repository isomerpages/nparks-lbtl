<p>📅 19 September – 27 September 2026
<br>📍 Sunken Garden, Japanese Garden | Cloud Pagoda, Chinese Garden</p>
<h4><strong>Sunken Garden Light Display</strong></h4>
<p>⏰7:30pm – 10:30pm</p>
<p>A vibrant wash of multicoloured lights transforms the Sunken Garden into
a magical after-dark spectacle, dotted with delicate lotus lanterns that
add a touch of festive warmth.</p>
<h4><strong>Cloud Pagoda Light Show</strong></h4>
<p>⏰ 7:30pm – 10pm, every 30 minutes (last show at 10pm)</p>
<p>The enduring tale of Bai Suzhen, Xu Xian, Xiao Qing, and Fa Hai has touched
countless hearts with its themes of love, sacrifice, and redemption. Watch
their story come to life in a stunning light show at the Cloud Pagoda —
and in this reimagined ending, the four characters are finally reunited,
setting aside past grievances to embrace forgiveness and harmony. A beautiful
reminder that love and openness can overcome all.</p>
<p>On 21 September, the Cloud Pagoda will also glow in yellow and purple
in honour of World Alzheimer's Day.</p>
